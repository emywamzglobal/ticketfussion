Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the questions we receive most often about buying, selling, and managing tickets on TicketFussion.

How will I receive my ticket?

Once your payment has been successfully confirmed, your electronic ticket will be delivered to you and will also be available in your account if you choose to create one.

Do I need an account to buy tickets?

No. You can complete your purchase as a guest. Creating an account is optional and allows you to keep your purchase history in one place.

Can I sell my tickets on TicketFussion?

Yes. TicketFussion allows individuals and businesses to list tickets for eligible events through the seller portal.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Refunds and cancellations depend on the event organiser's policy. Please review the event information before completing your purchase.

What payment methods are accepted?

Available payment options are displayed securely during checkout.

How do I contact TicketFussion?

Visit our Contact page or reach us through our official TikTok account @ticketfussion.
