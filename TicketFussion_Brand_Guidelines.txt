================================================== OFFICIAL BRAND COLORS
==================================================

Primary Blue HEX: #006CEE RGB: 0, 108, 238

Purpose: - Primary buttons - Links - Icons - Active navigation -
Highlights

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Primary Navy HEX: #021832 RGB: 2, 24, 50

Purpose: - Headings - Navigation - Footer - Dark backgrounds - Primary
text

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Neutral Gray HEX: #666666 RGB: 102, 102, 102

Purpose: - Secondary text - Event details - Dates - Locations -
Descriptions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

White HEX: #FFFFFF

Purpose: - Backgrounds - Cards - Forms

================================================== SUPPORTING UI COLORS
==================================================

Background HEX: #F8FAFC

Border HEX: #E5E7EB

Success HEX: #22C55E

================================================== TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================

Headings: Poppins (Bold / SemiBold)

Body: Inter (Regular / Medium)

================================================== BRAND RULES
==================================================

• Use #006CEE for primary actions. • Use #021832 for premium sections
and headings. • Keep backgrounds light and clean. • Avoid introducing
additional brand colors. • Maintain a modern, minimal interface. • Build
every page with consistency.
